## Overview

Jeremy's Bible App is a personal Bible reading and journaling app. Your notes, annotations, and reading progress are stored in your own Google Drive — the developer has no server, no backend, and no access to your data.

---

## Information We Collect

### Google Account Information

When you sign in with Google, the app receives your Google account **email address**. This is used only to identify which Google Drive account to sync with. No other Google account information (name, profile photo, contacts, etc.) is accessed.

### Reading Progress and Preferences

The app stores your current reading location (book, chapter, verse), the chapters you have marked as read, and your in-app preferences (font size, theme, display language, etc.).

### User-Created Content

The app stores content you create:

- **Annotations** — text notes, highlights, bookmarks, and handwritten ink strokes attached to specific Bible verses.
- **Journals** — handwritten ink entries organized by Bible chapter.

---

## How Your Data Is Stored

**On your device:** All data is saved locally so the app works offline.

**On Google Drive:** If you sign in with Google, your reading progress, preferences, annotations, and journals are synced to a private folder in your own Google Drive (`appDataFolder`). This folder:

- Is **not visible** in the Google Drive file browser.
- Is **not accessible** to any other app.
- Is **not accessible** to the developer — only you and Google can read it.

The developer does not operate any server or database. Your data never passes through any system controlled by the developer.

---

## How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to:

- Display your Bible reading content and annotations within the app.
- Sync your data across your devices via your Google Drive.
- Restore your data when you reinstall the app.

The app does **not** use your data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any purpose beyond the features you see in the app.

---

## Data Sharing

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party, with one exception: your data is synced to **Google Drive** as described above. Google's storage and handling of data in your Drive is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

No other third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, or crash reporting services are used in this app.

---

## Google's Role

This app uses Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API. By signing in with Google, you also agree to [Google's Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms) and [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time via your [Google Account permissions page](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

---

## Your Rights and Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time:

1. **Delete app data from Google Drive:** Go to [Google Drive Storage settings](https://drive.google.com/drive/storage) → Manage → find "Jeremy's Bible App" → Delete app data.
2. **Revoke Google access:** Go to [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) → find "Jeremy's Bible App" → Remove access.
3. **Delete local data:** Uninstall the app from your device.

After deletion, the developer retains no copy of your data.
